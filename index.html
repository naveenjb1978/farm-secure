🌱 Crop Management Tips

  • Rotate crops regularly to maintain soil fertility.
  • Use organic compost to improve soil health.
  • Adopt drip irrigation to save water and increase yield.
  • Monitor for pests weekly and use biocontrol methods when possible.

☀️ Weather Awareness

Plan sowing and harvesting based on local weather forecasts. Avoid applying fertilizers or pesticides during heavy rain periods.
